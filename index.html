Day-Old Discount

Whatever's left after 3pm goes half off — ask at the counter, we don't advertise it loudly but we're not precious about it either.

Large Orders

Dozens, office trays, or whole cakes need 48 hours' notice. Call ahead rather than walking in for these.

Allergens

All items are made in a kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, eggs, and tree nuts. Ask staff for full ingredient lists.
